1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ai-media-editor is an unprivileged, local-first media preparation and editing
orchestration platform tailored for autonomous AI coding agents (Claude Code,
Gemini/Antigravity, Codex).

Key Value Proposition:
- Replaces proprietary cloud STT services (such as ElevenLabs Scribe) with 100%
  offline speech-to-text engines (faster-whisper and WhisperX) producing schema-
  compatible Scribe JSON outputs.
- Empowers multimodal LLMs and coding agents to visually judge and edit video
  recordings through deterministic timestamped frame contact-sheets and
  pause-aware cut candidate lists.
- Integrated offline procedural score synthesis via tools/compose_music.py using
  numpy waveforms driven by storyline emotion and intensity parameters.
- Suppresses disruptive console window bursts under Windows via preloaded child
  process wrappers (tools/hide-windows.cjs, tools/hf.cmd).

2. TARGET AUDIENCES & PERSONAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Autonomous Agent Developers:
   Engineers orchestrating agentic video production pipelines that require

2. Local-First Podcasters & Content Creators:
   Creators demanding zero network egress for raw voice/video recordings,
   eliminating data leaks and third-party subscription overhead.
3. Open-Source AI Multimedia Pioneers:
   Developers combining Hyperframes (HTML/CSS/JS -> MP4 animations), video-use,
   and open STT models on consumer hardware.
