1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ai-media-editor is an unprivileged, local-first media preparation, transcription,
and editing orchestration platform engineered specifically for autonomous AI
coding agents (Claude Code, Gemini/Antigravity, Codex CLI, Kimi Code).

Key Value Proposition:
- Replaces proprietary cloud STT services (such as ElevenLabs Scribe) with 100%
  offline, zero-egress speech-to-text engines (faster-whisper and WhisperX) that
  produce schema-valid Scribe JSON outputs.
- Empowers multimodal LLMs and coding agents to visually judge and edit video
  recordings through deterministic timestamped frame contact-sheets and
  pause-aware cut candidate lists without SaaS credential friction.
- Built-in offline procedural music synthesis (tools/compose_music.py) using pure
  NumPy waveforms driven by storyline emotion, intensity, and timing parameters.
- Suppresses disruptive console window bursts under Windows via preloaded child
  process wrappers (tools/hide-windows.cjs, tools/hf.cmd).
- Fully decoupled, zero-cloud architecture with 10 strict runtime invariants.

2. TARGET AUDIENCES & PERSONAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[PERSONA-01] Autonomous AI Coding Agent Developers & Swarm Architects:
   Role: Engineers orchestrating agentic video and podcast production pipelines.
   Pain Point: Cloud SaaS requires API keys, leaks confidential pre-release content,
   suffers rate limits, and lacks agent-native structured JSON cut interfaces.
   Solution: Deterministic CLI orchestration, Scribe JSON schema generation, and
   frame contact-sheets designed specifically for LLM vision models.
   High-Intent Query: "agent driven video editor with local transcription"

[PERSONA-02] Local-First Podcasters & Content Creators:
   Role: Independent podcasters, interviewers, and media producers.
   Pain Point: Monthly recurring SaaS subscriptions, vendor lock-in, and forced
   cloud uploading of multi-gigabyte raw audio/video files.
   Solution: 100% offline faster-whisper STT, pause detection, speaker diarization,
   and automated vinyl/waveform animated cover generation.
   High-Intent Query: "local first podcast editing AI agent workflow"

[PERSONA-03] AI Video & Motion Graphics Engineers:
   Role: Motion designers and technical animators building programmatic video.
   Pain Point: Complex GUI timeline editors (Premiere, After Effects) cannot be
   automated via code or LLM prompts without fragile desktop macros.
   Solution: Hyperframes integration translating HTML/CSS/JavaScript directly
   into 60fps MP4 lower-thirds, title cards, and animated callouts.
   High-Intent Query: "Hyperframes motion graphics podcast editor"

[PERSONA-04] Enterprise Media Security, Privacy & Compliance Officers:
   Role: Legal, security, and compliance officers in healthcare, finance, and enterprise.
   Pain Point: Strict regulatory mandates (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC-2) prohibiting cloud
   transmission of internal all-hands, earnings briefings, and patient recordings.
   Solution: Verifiable zero-egress guarantee, unprivileged RunAsInvoker non-elevation,
   Level 1 SBOM, and a formal 48h vulnerability response SLA.
   High-Intent Query: "zero egress video transcription and cutting"
