1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & COMPLIANCE ASSURANCE
================================================================================

ai-media-editor is engineered under strict architectural and governance invariants:
100% Local-First, Zero-Egress by default, unprivileged user-mode execution (RunAsInvoker),
and pure offline operation. The media preparation, local speech-to-text routing
(faster-whisper, WhisperX), Scribe JSON schema generation, frame extraction, pause
detection, and procedural music synthesis operate entirely within local process and
filesystem boundaries without unsolicited network communication or telemetry.

All direct, runtime, optional, and development dependencies utilized across ai-media-editor
are distributed under strictly permissive and free open-source licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0,
BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, PSFL-2.0) or dynamically linked media utilities (FFmpeg LGPL-2.1+).
There are ZERO AGPL or viral copyleft constraints, ensuring maximum portability and compliance
for autonomous AI coding agents, enterprise media workflows, and local podcast production.

3. ZERO-COPYLEFT ISOLATION GUARANTEE & RUNASINVOKER CERTIFICATION
================================================================================

1. Zero-Copyleft Isolation Guarantee:
   All external multimedia utilities (such as FFmpeg) are accessed strictly across CLI
   subprocess boundaries (subprocess.run / subprocess.Popen) using standard streams. No
   component of ai-media-editor links against, vendors, or invokes any code under GPLv1,
   GPLv2, GPLv3, AGPLv3, SSPL, or CC-BY-SA copyleft licenses. All dependencies and tools
   are strictly permissive (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, PSFL) or dynamically invoked CLI utilities
   (FFmpeg LGPL-2.1+). This preserves complete dynamic link isolation: no copyleft viral
   contamination affects downstream consumer code.

2. Unprivileged Execution (RunAsInvoker):
   ai-media-editor requires no administrative privileges, no daemon services, no root
   credentials, and no kernel-level device drivers. It operates entirely in unprivileged
   user space under standard user profiles.

3. Zero-Egress Perimeter:
   By default, no network traffic is emitted by ai-media-editor. All transcription models,
   waveform computations, and frame contact sheets are processed on local compute.
   Controlled remote compute via SSH is strictly opt-in and governed by isolated job
   directories and mandatory remote cleanup.
